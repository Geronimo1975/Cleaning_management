Help & Guidelines
Creating Compliant Contracts

Follow these guidelines when creating contracts to ensure compliance with German labor laws:

  • Clear Job Description: Include specific duties and responsibilities.
  • Working Hours: Specify regular working hours, break times, and overtime agreements.
  • Compensation: Clearly define hourly rates or monthly salary, along with any bonuses or benefits.
  • Notice Period: Include appropriate notice periods for termination (typically 4 weeks).
  • Probation Period: If applicable, include a probation period (up to 6 months).
Required Terms for Different Contract Types

Depending on the contract type, different terms may be required:

  • Full-time/Part-time: Standard employment rights, including paid leave, sick pay, and social security contributions.
  • Mini-job: For jobs paying up to €520 per month, with simplified tax and social security.
  • Freelance: Specify service scope, payment terms, and clarify self-employment status to avoid pseudo-employment issues.
