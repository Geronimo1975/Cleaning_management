Help & Tips
Template Variables

You can use the following placeholders in your template:

  • {{cleaner_name}} - Full name of the cleaner
  • {{company_name}} - Name of the company
  • {{contract_number}} - Unique contract identifier
  • {{start_date}} - Contract start date
  • {{end_date}} - Contract end date
  • {{hourly_rate}} - Hourly payment rate
  • {{monthly_salary}} - Monthly salary amount
Creating Good Templates
